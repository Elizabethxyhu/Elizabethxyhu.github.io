## About Me

<p>
  Hi, I am currently a Postdoctoral Associate at the
  Department of Data and Systems Engineering at the
  University of Hong Kong (HKU), supervised by
  <a href="https://zj-maxshen.github.io/">Prof. Max Shen</a> and
  <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/shaochong/">Prof. Shaochong Lin</a>.
  Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Decisions,
  Operations and Technology at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK),
  supervised by
  <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/z-chen">Prof. Zhi Chen</a>.
  I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from CUHK,
  advised by
  <a href="http://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/~jlee/">Prof. Jimmy H. M. Lee</a>.
</p>

<p>
  My research lies at the intersection of
  <strong><em>AI</em></strong> and
  <strong><em>Optimization</em></strong>, focusing on developing machine
  learning methods for decision-making and solving complex real-world
  optimization problems.
  I have published <strong><em>10+ papers</em></strong> in top-tier conferences
  and journals, including <strong>oral and spotlight presentations</strong>,
  and serve as an
  <strong><em>Area Chair (AC) for ICLR and ICML</em></strong>.
</p>

## Research Interests

<ul>
  <li">
    <strong><em><span style="color: darkred;">Decision-Focused Learning (Predict+Optimize).</span></em></strong>
    Developing learning frameworks that directly optimize downstream decision
    quality, integrating machine learning with mathematical optimization under
    unknown objective and constraint parameters, discrete decisions, and
    multi-stage information revelation.
  </li>

  <li">
    <strong><em><span style="color: darkred;">Causal Learning and Decision Calibration.</span></em></strong>
    Developing causal calibration methods that leverage limited experimental
    data and observational information to improve prediction reliability under
    hidden confounding and partial experimental coverage, and translate causal
    corrections into better optimization decisions.
  </li>

  <li">
    <strong><em><span style="color: darkred;">Exogenous-Aware Time Series Forecasting.</span></em></strong>
    Developing data-driven forecasting methods that capture periodic patterns,
    external events, and heterogeneous temporal dynamics, with applications to
    demand forecasting and complex spatiotemporal systems.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong><em><span style="color: darkred;">Contextual and Stochastic Optimization.</span></em></strong>
    Integrating contextual prediction with risk- and cost-aware optimization
    to support resource allocation and operational decisions under demand
    uncertainty and asymmetric business costs.
  </li>
</ul>
